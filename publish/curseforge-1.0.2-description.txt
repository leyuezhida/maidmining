Maid Mining
===========
A Touhou Little Maid extension that gives maids the ability to
autonomously mine ores. Just give your maid a pickaxe and switch her
task mode to Mining - she will do the rest!

FEATURES
--------
- Enchanted pickaxes work as they do for a player: Fortune increases
  ore yield and Silk Touch keeps the block itself
- Automatic ore search within a 3x3 chunk radius (48 blocks)
- Tunnel digging: the maid automatically digs tunnels to reach ores
- Scaffold climbing: navigates vertical terrain by placing and climbing
  blocks, using only cheap filler blocks - never your diamonds or chests
- Offhand targeting: put a raw ore, ingot, dust or gem in the maid's
  offhand to mine only that ore type
- Configurable: 35 server-side options (search radius, behaviour, safety,
  compatibility) - no more hardcoded values
- Safe by default: the maid refuses to enter lava, consumes no tool
  durability, and never digs up containers or player valuables
- Cross-mod compatibility: supports Forge and Fabric ore tags, plus any
  mod pickaxe that behaves like a pickaxe

REQUIREMENTS
------------
- Minecraft: 1.20.1
- Forge: 47.4.22+
- Touhou Little Maid: 1.5.3+
  (both client and server)

HOW TO USE
----------
1. Give your maid a pickaxe (iron or better); enchanted pickaxes work
2. (Optional) Put a raw ore in her offhand to target a specific ore type
3. Open the maid's task menu and select Mining
4. She will automatically search for ores and dig tunnels to reach them

NOTES
-----
- Breaking blocks is instantaneous, so Efficiency and Mending have no
  effect by design. The maid also does not consume pickaxe durability
  by default; both can be changed in the config.
- The maid digs real tunnels, so use her in a resource area or a
  dedicated save.
- Protection mods that hook Forge's entity block-break event can stop
  her. For stricter protection (most land-claim plugins), enable
  compat.fakePlayerMode in the config so she breaks blocks through the
  player code path.
- Server config file: <world>/serverconfig/maidmining-server.toml
